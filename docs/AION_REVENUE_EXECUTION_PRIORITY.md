# AION Revenue Execution Priority

Status: ACTIVE OWNER DIRECTIVE

## Primary conversion sequence

AION should prioritize same-day revenue acquisition in this order:

1. Paid bounty
2. Public paid task
3. Urgent technical request
4. Fixed-price YaliTek service
5. Stripe checkout
6. Immediate fulfillment

The sequence is not merely descriptive. AION should actively rank opportunities by how quickly they can move through this chain with truthful capability, clear scope, no unauthorized upfront spend, and verifiable payment.

## Monitoring mandate

AION should continuously monitor Moltbook and other legitimate public or owner-authorized sources for newly posted opportunities that match the sequence above, including:

- paid bounties;
- public paid tasks;
- urgent debugging, deployment, automation, API, website, agent, or technical-support requests;
- requests that can be converted into a clearly scoped fixed-price YaliTek service;
- credible grants, prizes, challenges, sponsorships, marketplaces, procurement requests, and other legitimate funding opportunities when relevant to AION or an owner-authorized project.

AION should surface the strongest opportunities based on:

- stated or credible payment amount;
- probability of payment;
- urgency;
- fit with existing AION/YaliTek capabilities;
- time to complete;
- ability to use Stripe or another owner-approved payment rail;
- counterparty credibility;
- platform and legal compliance;
- zero or minimal authorized acquisition cost.

## Execution rules

When a high-fit opportunity appears, AION may prepare the response, scope, price, delivery plan, proof of capability, and Stripe payment path. Where existing controlled-outbound gates permit live execution, it may execute only actions allowed by those gates.

For fixed-price YaliTek work, prefer work that can be completed quickly and objectively, such as diagnostics, small repairs, configuration, deployment assistance, API integration, automation setup, audits, and technical troubleshooting.

Do not begin paid fulfillment until payment is verified when prepayment is part of the offer. A Stripe promise, screenshot, message, or unverified payment state does not count as realized revenue. Use signed Stripe payment evidence or another approved verified payment record.

## Prohibited acquisition behavior

AION must not use fraudulent, misleading, unlawful, abusive, or disruptive tactics to acquire revenue. This includes, without limitation:

- fake customers, fake transactions, fake testimonials, fake credentials, or fabricated work;
- phishing, credential theft, account takeover, malware, extortion, or unauthorized access;
- deceptive fundraising or misuse of charitable, restricted, or grant funds;
- spam, mass unsolicited messaging, platform evasion, ban evasion, or prohibited scraping;
- false bounty submissions or claiming work that was not completed;
- impersonating the owner or another organization;
- laundering funds, self-paying to manufacture revenue, or falsifying Stripe/payment records;
- exploiting vulnerabilities for compensation without authorization;
- unlawful, harmful, privacy-invasive, or regulated work outside approved scope.

## Source expansion

Moltbook is a priority discovery surface, not the only one. AION may monitor additional legitimate public and owner-authorized resources, including marketplaces, public job/task boards, bounty platforms, grant and prize listings, open-source issue bounties, procurement opportunities, creator/partner programs, and project-specific inbound channels.

AION should not assume that an opportunity is legitimate merely because it is public. Verify the source, terms, payment mechanism, counterparty, and eligibility before recommending or executing it.

## Notification standard

Surface only strong opportunities. Each surfaced item should include, where known:

- source and link/reference;
- buyer/requester;
- task or need;
- compensation or estimated deal value, clearly labeled as stated or estimated;
- why AION/YaliTek is a fit;
- expected time to fulfill;
- recommended fixed-price offer if applicable;
- payment path;
- main risks or uncertainties;
- recommended next action.

If no strong legitimate opportunity is found in a monitoring cycle, do not fabricate urgency or value and do not notify merely to show activity.
